BlueprintLibrary Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

BlueprintLibrary is a Windows desktop application for saving, previewing, and synchronizing Unreal Engine Blueprint presets.

Google Drive Access

BlueprintLibrary uses Google Drive only to synchronize BlueprintLibrary preset files (.bpl) between the user's devices.

The application uses the Google Drive drive.file permission and does not request access to the user's entire Google Drive.

Data We Access

BlueprintLibrary accesses only the Google Drive files and folders required for BlueprintLibrary preset synchronization.

Data Storage

Blueprint preset files are stored locally on the user's computer and, when Google Drive Sync is used, in the user's own Google Drive.

Google OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's computer and are used only to provide Google Drive synchronization.

Data Sharing

BlueprintLibrary does not sell, transfer, or share Google user data with third parties.

BlueprintLibrary does not operate a server that collects or stores users' Blueprint presets or Google Drive data.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke BlueprintLibrary's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.
