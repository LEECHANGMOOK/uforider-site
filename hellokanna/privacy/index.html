HelloKanna Privacy Policy

UFORiderSTUDIO operates HelloKanna. Updated November 4, 2026.

Learning data and cloud backup

HelloKanna stores learning progress, collected cards, saved words and their ratings, and app preferences on your device. You can study without signing in. If you use cloud backup or restore, Google Sign-In authenticates you through Firebase Authentication. Firebase creates an app account and assigns it a unique user ID. HelloKanna uses that ID to save and find your backup in Cloud Firestore. The backup contains learning progress, collected cards, saved words, and word ratings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use HelloKanna and improve it. It processes app instance identifiers and app activity, such as onboarding steps, lesson starts and completions, and interactions around ads.

Advertising

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general location, device or account identifiers, interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Ad behavior depends on your region and applicable privacy choices.

Purchases

Google Play processes in-app purchases. HelloKanna uses purchase information provided by Google Play, including product, purchase status and purchase token, to unlock paid features, acknowledge purchases, and restore access. HelloKanna does not receive your payment card details.

Service providers and security

Google provides sign-in, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, and billing services as described above. Data sent to these services is protected in transit. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Retention and deletion

We keep your HelloKanna account and cloud backup to make backup and restore available. When we verify and complete an account deletion request, we delete the account and its cloud backup. Google Play manages your Google account and purchase history separately. You can remove HelloKanna data stored on your device by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings.

Request deletion of your HelloKanna account and cloud backup.

Contact

If you contact us, we use your email address and the information in your message to respond and handle your request. For privacy questions or requests, contact UFORiderSTUDIO at uforiderstudio00@gmail.com.
