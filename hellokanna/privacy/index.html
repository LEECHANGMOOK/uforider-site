HelloKanna Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Last updated: November 1, 2026

UFORiderSTUDIO (“we,” “us,” or “the operator”) explains below how information is handled in connection with HelloKanna, our Japanese learning app, including its purposes of use, retention, and deletion.

App: HelloKanna (헬로칸나)
Android package: com.uforiderstudio.hellokanna

The current app does not require an account. Learning records, collection cards, and personal notes are stored on your device. The app does not transmit this information to our servers. If you contact us by email, we process the information in your inquiry to respond and help resolve your issue.

1. Information Stored on Your Device and Its Purposes

The app stores the following information on your device to provide its learning features:

  • Learning progress, best scores, completion status, study counts, and vocabulary review records: to let you continue studying and display your progress.
  • Card ownership and acquisition order, reward claim records, and new-card viewing status: to manage your collection and prevent duplicate rewards.
  • Notes you write for collection cards: to display your personal learning notes.
  • Volume and learning settings: to retain your preferences.

This information is not uploaded to our servers or made available to other users. The current app does not provide account registration, account-based cloud synchronization, advertising, or a separate usage analytics feature.

Please do not enter sensitive information, such as passwords or national identification numbers, in card notes.

2. Personal Information Processed When You Contact Us

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, the name displayed in your email, your inquiry, and any materials you choose to attach. We use this information to review and respond to your inquiry, investigate errors, and handle requests concerning your privacy rights.

We use information only to the extent needed to handle your inquiry and do not request unnecessary personal information. Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, or other unnecessary personal information. Before attaching screenshots or files, check whether they contain other personal information.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Information on your device: This information remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Settings labels may vary by device. The app does not provide its own cloud backup or server-based recovery service.
  • Inquiry information: We delete inquiry information within 90 days after the inquiry has been resolved. If retention is required by applicable law or necessary to handle a dispute, we retain only the relevant information for the necessary period and delete it when that purpose ends.

We cannot remotely access or delete learning records or card notes stored on your device on your behalf. When the retention purpose has ended, we delete electronic information we hold in a manner intended to make recovery difficult.

4. External Services and Information Sharing

We do not sell your learning records or card notes or share them with third parties for advertising purposes.

Google Play

App installation, updates, and diagnostic information processed by Google Play itself are governed by Google’s policies and your settings. We may use error and app quality statistics available through Google Play Console to assess app stability. This is separate from operating an additional analytics SDK within the app.

More information: Google Privacy Policy

Email Inquiries

We receive and respond to inquiries through Gmail. Google may process inquiry information as part of delivering and storing email. Processing locations and methods are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy Webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This page itself contains no advertising, visitor analytics code, input forms, or tracking cookies added by us.

More information: GitHub Pages Data Collection · GitHub Privacy Statement

5. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You can delete app data through your device settings. For inquiry information we hold, you may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing by contacting the email address below.

A legal representative or other authorized representative may also submit a request. We handle requests in accordance with applicable law after performing the minimum verification necessary to confirm the requester’s authority. If legal restrictions prevent us from fulfilling a request, we will explain the reason.

The app does not provide user accounts, so there is no separate account closure process. If a parent or guardian is concerned that a child’s personal information was included in an inquiry, they may contact us to request deletion or other assistance.

6. Information Protection Measures

We restrict access to inquiry information to those who need it and apply safeguards such as account security and access management. To help protect information stored on your device, please keep your device screen lock enabled and your operating system up to date.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If our information handling practices change, we will update this page and its revision date. Important changes will be announced through the app or this page. If we introduce advertising, payments, accounts, or online features in the future, we will update the policy to reflect the actual processing involved and provide additional notices and obtain consent where required by applicable law.
