What is deleted

We delete your HelloKanna sign-in account and its cloud backup, including lesson progress, collected cards, saved words, and word ratings. We do not keep these records after completing the request.

Your Google account and Google Play purchase records are managed by Google. To remove learning data stored on your device, clear the app storage in Android Settings.
